Meet Dr. Vee

Dr. Victoria Verlezza (ver-LEZ-uh) is a cognitive equity strategist based in Boston. With 20+ years supporting individuals and organizations, she helps teams, especially engineering firms and STEAM workplaces, build cultures that are equity-driven in practice, not only in name.

She holds a PhD in Human Development, plus master’s degrees in Social Justice Education, Higher Education Administration, and Human Development. As a scholar-practitioner with lived experience, she brings both expertise and empathy to neurodivergence and systemic change.

She has spoken with the ACLU. She teaches. She facilitates nationally. She is also the person who will make a room of executives laugh about their own meeting culture before asking them to redesign it.

HR, DEI, L&D, education, and non-profits are part of the work too. Engineering and STEAM are the focus, not a fence.
